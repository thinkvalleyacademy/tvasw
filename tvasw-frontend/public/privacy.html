Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Thinkvalley Softwares ("Thinkvalley", "we", "us") collects through thinkvalleysoftwares.in, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Thinkvalley Softwares is operated by Think Valley Softwares, Whitefield, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

1. Summary

  • We collect only what we need to answer you and to work on your software requirements.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Your AI chat is sent to Google Gemini to create replies (see section 4).
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

2. What we collect

When you contact us

Your name, email address and message from the contact form.

When you create an account

Your name and email address, and your password. We store the password only as a one-way BCrypt hash, so nobody, including us, can read it.

When you use the AI requirement assistant

The project details you enter (name, type, summary, timeline, budget, preferred technologies), your chat messages and the assistant's replies, the requirement documents created from them, and the project's status.

Technical data

Like every website, our servers and our network provider receive your IP address and browser details when you visit. We use IP addresses briefly, in memory, to limit repeated sign-in attempts and form submissions; we do not build profiles from them. Our servers keep technical logs of events and errors, which are rotated automatically.

We process your data with your consent, which you give by submitting the contact form or creating an account, and for these stated purposes. You can withdraw consent by asking us to delete your data (section 7); this does not affect processing that already took place.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Gemini API) receives your project description and chat messages to generate the assistant's replies and documents. Google handles this data under its own terms for the Gemini API. Google may also use this content to improve its products and services, and its trained reviewers may read it. Please do not type passwords, payment details or other people's personal data into the chat.
  • Cloudflare carries all traffic to our website, protects it from attacks and provides HTTPS. It processes your IP address and request details and may set a strictly necessary security cookie.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on our pages, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.
  • Our email provider handles email you send us or that we send you.

Our own team sees your account details, and sees your project, chat and documents once you send them to us. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others. We never sell personal data.

5. Where your data is stored

Our application and database run on servers located in India. Cloudflare and Google operate worldwide, so data they process may be handled outside India.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, projects, chats and documents: while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete this data, except anything we must keep by law.
  • Contact form messages: for up to 2 years, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Backups: daily copies kept for 14 days, so deleted data disappears from backups within 14 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or complete inaccurate data;
  • delete your data and account;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to;
  • resolve a complaint (section 10).

You can delete your account and all its projects yourself: log in, open "My projects" and choose "Delete my account". For other requests, email us from the address on your account and we will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

8. Security

We protect your data with HTTPS-only connections, Cloudflare protection, an encrypted tunnel with no public server ports, a private VPN for administration, hashed passwords, per-account access control, rate limits and daily backups. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. When you log in, your browser keeps your login in session storage, which is removed when you close the tab or log out. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie.

10. Children

Our services are for adults and businesses. You must be 18 or older to create an account. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top, and if a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell account holders by email or on the website before it applies.
