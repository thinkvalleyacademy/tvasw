Security

A Secure Environment at Every Step

Your ideas and data deserve protection. Here is how our own platform is secured, in plain words.

Protected by Cloudflare

Visitors reach our platform through the Cloudflare network, which filters attack traffic before it gets to our server.

No Public Server Ports

The website is published through an encrypted Cloudflare Tunnel. The application listens only inside the server, not on the open internet.

Private VPN for Our Team

We manage our servers over Tailscale, an encrypted private network, so administration stays separate from public traffic.

Encrypted Connections

Our website works over HTTPS only. Passwords are stored as one-way BCrypt hashes, so nobody can read them, including us.

Your Project Stays Private

Every client has their own account and can open only their own projects and chats. Our team sees your requirements once you send them.

Backups and Abuse Limits

Platform data is backed up every day, and sign-in, forms and AI requests are rate limited to stop abuse and password guessing.

How your data travels

  1. Your browserEncrypted with HTTPS
  2. CloudflareFilters and protects traffic
  3. Secure tunnelOutbound only, no open ports
  4. Our applicationLogin, limits and private data

Our team manages the server through a separate route: the Tailscale private VPN.

Questions

Security and Privacy FAQ

Who can see my project?

Other clients cannot open your project. When you click "Send to Thinkvalley", our team receives your document and chat so we can review your requirements. Authorised administrators maintain the server where the data is stored.

Does the AI assistant share my information?

The assistant uses Google Gemini, so your project description and chat messages are sent to Google to generate replies. Google may also use them to improve its services, and its reviewers may read them. Please do not type passwords, bank details or other people's personal data into the chat.

Do I need a VPN to use this website?

No. You only need a browser. Our team uses the Tailscale VPN to manage servers, and we can set up private access for your own staff as part of a project.

How do I report a security problem?

Email thinkvalleyacademy@gmail.com with the subject "Security report". Please do not include passwords or other people's data.
